classical Collatz orbit (odd n -> 3n+1, even n -> n/2) with one extra point 2(3n+1) inserted after
every odd term; the odd-to-odd map is exactly the Syracuse map n -> (3n+1)/2^v.
Hence a T-orbit is bounded/eventually periodic iff the corresponding Collatz orbit is.

point), ending in the cycle 1 -> 8 -> 4 -> 2 -> 1.

exactly the "no divergent orbit" half). Descent arguments, congruence invariants and finite automata
all fail for the same reasons they fail for 3n+1 (only density results such as Terras/Tao "almost all"
are known). Not claimed beyond simulation.
