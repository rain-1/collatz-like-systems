which merges with the U-orbit of 7: FATE is exactly "does 7 diverge under 5x+1" (par_5_1_s7), OPEN.
(Seed 27 cannot lie on a cycle anyway: 27 ∉ 5Z, and all orbits stay in 5Z after step 1.)
