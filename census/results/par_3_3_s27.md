## Conjugacy (tier 1, proved)
3Z is forward invariant (halving an even multiple of 3 stays in 3Z; 3n+3 = 3(n+1)),
and on it F(3m) = 3C(m) with C the Collatz map (3m odd iff m odd; 3(3m)+3 = 3(3m+1)).
Every positive orbit hits an odd number within finitely many halvings and its image
is in 3Z. Hence every F-orbit is eventually 3 x (a Collatz orbit), and conversely every
Collatz orbit of m appears as the F-orbit of 3m.
