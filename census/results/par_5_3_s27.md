## Reduction (tier 1, proved)
3Z is invariant (3 | n => 3 | 5n+3, 3 | n/2) and T(3m) = 3*U(m), U the 5x+1 map (15m+3 = 3(5m+1)).
27 = 3*9, so the T-orbit of 27 is exactly 3 times the U-orbit of 9, which is the U-orbit of 7 after
3 steps (7 -> 36 -> 18 -> 9). So FATE here is exactly the open problem in par_5_1_s7.
Also the complement of 3Z is invariant (n !≡ 0 mod 3 => 5n+3 ≡ 2n !≡ 0, n/2 !≡ 0), so 5x+3 splits into
the 5x+1 problem on 3Z plus a separate 5x+3 problem on gcd(n,3)=1. GLOBAL "yes" would imply 5x+1 global
"yes"; a proof that 7 diverges under 5x+1 would give GLOBAL "no". Both open.
