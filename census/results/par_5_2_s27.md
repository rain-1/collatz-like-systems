## Proof
If n is odd then 5n is odd and 5n+2 is odd, and 5n+2 > n. So from any odd term the
orbit is odd forever and strictly increasing; explicitly 2T^k(n)+1 = (2n+1)5^k.
A strictly increasing sequence never repeats, so the orbit is infinite.
Seed 27 is odd: FATE = infinite.
Every positive n reaches an odd number after v_2(n) halvings, after which it
increases strictly; hence no positive orbit is eventually periodic. GLOBAL = no.
