## GLOBAL
"Every positive orbit is eventually periodic" is the no-divergent-trajectory half of
the Collatz (3x+1) conjecture: open. Known partial results: verified computationally to
beyond 2^68 (Oliveira e Silva; Barina extended further); Terras/Everett density-one
descent; Tao (2019) almost all orbits attain almost bounded values (logarithmic
density). None gives all n. Descent+finite table fails because the residue classes
needing more than k bits of lookahead never vanish; no finite automaton / congruence
invariant can work since it would settle Collatz.
