OPEN QUESTIONS IN THE COLLATZ-LIKE SYSTEMS CENSUS
Two-round snapshot | 66 tier-5 entries

Cite the CLS ID when sharing work on an entry. Each ID identifies a ranked
question and its census record. A map can have multiple questions, and
some questions were revisited in round 2. See also the cross-references.
An open label records the census status. The census used agent review
and computational checks and has had no human review.

All maps act on the positive integers. An arrow gives one iteration.
In a rule such as 3q+1 -> 4q+1, q is an integer and the input is positive.
Conditions with 'else' are evaluated in the listed order. An orbit is
eventually periodic if it enters a finite cycle, including a fixed point.

A sweep on 25 November 2026 (one attacking agent per cluster, one referee per
claim) resolved none of the open questions below. It proved exact equivalences
(29 items) that collapse most of the 3n+d, 5n+d and 1932-map entries onto a few core
problems, plus obstruction and restricted-cycle results. Entries it touched
carry a 'Sweep result' paragraph. Summary: census/lhf/README.md.
