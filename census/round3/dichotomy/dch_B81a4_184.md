## Proof

(H1), (H2) and (P) hold, so Theorem 1 of dichotomy.md applies. decide.py found integer weights c(r) for r mod 81 with c(s)·u_r < c(r)·w_r on every edge r → s of the residue graph. The weights have 60-bit size and are printed by `python3 decide.py --id dch_B81a4_184`. Each of the 135 edge inequalities is checked with integers, and the largest edge ratio is θ = 0.993351 < 1. By Theorem 2, V(n) = c(n mod 81)·n strictly decreases except on the finite set F = {n : V(Tn) ≥ V(n)}. F is contained in {n ≤ 149} (symbolic bounds N_r) and has 77 elements, the largest being 149. Every orbit meets F and every cycle meets F. Iterating each element of F until it repeats gives exactly the cycles listed above. ∎
