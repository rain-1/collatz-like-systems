## Why the one-run cycle method of CLS-048/053/051/052 does not apply
That method needs a single expanding affine branch whose p-adic fixed point is not a positive integer. Then a rising run of length k forces the cycle minimum n_0 to be at least c^k, and Baker's bound becomes exponentially strong. Here the expanding steps are (5n+1)/2 and (5n+1)/4, with 2-adic fixed points -1/3 and -1. The rising word fixes n_0 modulo 2^{V+1}, but which class that is depends on the word, and its least positive member can be small. With only Λ ≤ O(J)/n_0 and no exponential lower bound on n_0, the LMN bound does not limit the cycle length. A one-run classification would need a different idea, for example a Simons–de Weger-type LLL reduction together with a verified convergence range.
